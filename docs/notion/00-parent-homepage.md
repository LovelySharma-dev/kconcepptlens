## The story in four sentences

Students still learn heart chambers, organelles, and pointer mutations from flat ink. Unchecked generative tools can invent structures that feel authoritative. ConceptLens started from research into **how different learning needs collide with that status quo**, then narrowed the Hack Days build to lessons that can be **proved**: confirm text before explaining it, refuse a cyclic list, and deliver diagrams through Cloudinary transforms that serve Explore, Focus, and Low-data views of the same asset. Preferences are **choices**, not diagnoses; practice questions stay labeled as practice; Indian Sign Language video is not fabricated when no reviewed clip exists.
